1. Institutional Overview

The current reporting cycle reflects a steady trajectory in academic delivery and infrastructure utilization across the VNRVJIET campus. All primary academic departments (CSE, EEE, Mechanical, and Civil) have reported 98% attendance for the morning session. The transition to the new semester curriculum has been integrated into the digital learning management systems as per the standard protocol.

2. Departmental Performance

  • School of Computing: Successful completion of the International Symposium on AI Ethics with 12 guest lectures delivered by global research leads.
  • Engineering Sciences: Mid-term laboratory assessments concluded for the second-year cohort with an average score increase of 12% compared to the previous quarter.
  • Research & Development: Three new patent applications were filed today concerning sustainable energy harvesting in urban environments.

3. Faculty Developments

"The professional growth of our faculty remains the cornerstone of our academic excellence."

Today, six faculty members from the Department of Humanities attended a specialized workshop on 'Digital Pedagogy' hosted by the University of Oxford (Virtual). Internal appraisals for the upcoming accreditation cycle have commenced, with 45 dossiers submitted for peer review.
