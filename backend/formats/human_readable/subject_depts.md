# Subject Departments Daily Report Format

This format applies to the following departments:
- Chemistry
- English
- M&MS (Mathematics and Management Studies)

## Structure

### 1. Staff Attendance
- **Columns**: S.No, Dept., Category, On Rolls, Absent, Details/Remarks

### 2. Students Attendance (B.Tech / M.Tech / Minor Degree)
- **Columns**: Year, Rolls, Present, Absent

### 3. Infrastructure issues or Maintenance
- **Columns**: S.No., Description of the problem, Reported On, Attended On, Completed on, Remarks

### 4. Event organized
- **Columns**: S.No, Name of the Event, Duration, For Whom, No. of Participants, Resource Person/Faculty, Remarks

### 5. Participation by Staff/Students
- **Columns**: S. No, Name, Event, Status, Date and Duration

### 6. Staff joined or left
- **Columns**: S.No, Name, Designation, Date of Joining/leaving, Remarks

### 7. Classwork Adjustment
- **Columns**: S. No, Dept, Subject, Time, Adjusted from, Adjusted to

### 8. Incident if any
- **Columns**: S.No, Name, R. No. or ID No., Brief statement, Remarks
