# Consolidated Staff & Student Attendance Report Format

## Structure

### 1. Staff Attendance Table
- **Columns**: S.No, Dept., Category, On Rolls, Absent, DQI, DQI Performance, Overall DQI, Overall Performance

### 2. Reasons for Absence
- **Columns**: Sl No, Name of the Faculty, Reasons

### 3. Student Attendance Table
- **Columns**: Year, Particulars, CE, EEE, ME, ECE, CSE, EIE, IT, AE, CSBS, AIML, DS, IOT, CYS, AIDS
