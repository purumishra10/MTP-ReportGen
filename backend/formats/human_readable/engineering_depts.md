# Engineering Departments Daily Report Format

This format applies to the following departments:
- AE (Automobile Engineering)
- Civil Engineering
- CSE (Computer Science Engineering)
- CSE-(CyS, DS) and AI&DS
- CSE-AIML&IoT
- ECE (Electronics and Communication Engineering)
- EEE (Electrical and Electronics Engineering)
- EIE (Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering)
- IT (Information Technology)
- ME (Mechanical Engineering)

## Structure

### 1. Staff Attendance
- **Columns**: S.No, Dept., Category, On Rolls, Absent, Details/Remarks

### 2. Infrastructure issues or Maintenance
- **Columns**: S.No., Description of the problem, Reported On, Attended On, Completed on, Remarks

### 3. Event (short term course / workshops/Seminar, etc.) organized
- **Columns**: S.No, Name of the Event, Duration, For Whom, No. of Participants, Resource Person/Faculty, Remarks

### 4. Participation by Staff
- **Columns**: S. No, Name, Event, Status (delegate/paper/speaker), Date and Duration

### 5. Participation by Students
- **Columns**: S. No, Name, Event, Status (delegate/paper/speaker), Date and Duration

### 6. Staff joined or left
- **Columns**: S.No, Name of the Faculty, Designation, Date of Joining/leaving, Remarks

### 7. Classwork Adjustment / Lectures Interchanged
- **Columns**: S. No, Dept, Subject, Time, Adjusted from, Adjusted to

### 8. Incident if any
- **Columns**: S.No, Name, R. No. or ID No., Brief statement, Remarks
